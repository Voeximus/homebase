Finance
Home rebuilt

The hero is now a number that cannot lie to you. v1 said “safe to spend $531” — the envelope remainder, which would happily read $531 the day before $1,715 of rent left the account. It is now cash minus everything committed before the next payday minus your floor, and the subtraction is printed underneath rather than asserted. The envelopes are still here; they moved below the thing that can actually hurt you.

Insights real chart

v1's comparison bars encoded nothing — I set the widths by hand, which is the exact thing this app exists to stop doing. Each bar is now this cycle as a fraction of your own six-cycle average, with the 100% line drawn, so “+18%” is something you can see rather than something you have to take my word for.

Activity

Needs-review is a filter, not a pile, and a charge only lands there when the app genuinely cannot tell. Pending charges are marked inline instead of being swept in with them.
